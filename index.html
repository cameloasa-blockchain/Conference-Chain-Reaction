Why Chain Reaction?

More Than Technology

Blockchain is changing how we build, exchange and experience value. But innovation also brings new questions about trust, security and responsibility.

Chain Reaction brings together ideas and perspectives on the evolving Web3 landscape — from regulation and smart contract security to exchange transparency and decentralized finance.

One day. Five perspectives. A deeper look at what comes next.
